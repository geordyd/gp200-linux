# GP-200 firmware backup and loss review

This material describes limits. Only the current app owner's genuine capture
and your current review establish the backup available for this attempt.

Read the capture's coverage, failures and conditional consistency before entering
update mode. Presets, the current buffer, globals and metadata are retained only
to the extent shown by that acquisition. A local draft is separate from device
contents. Installed audio in all 20 user IR slots cannot be backed up through
this workflow. Slot names and local IR files do not prove installed audio.
SnapTone and other unretained device content cannot be claimed as restored.

Updating may change, erase or migrate contents. Retaining a source archive does
not prove preservation, successful restoration or equality after the update.
Completing firmware transfer does not verify user data or installed IR audio.
Keep your own original IR and preset files and any other available backups.

Entering firmware-update mode may require a complete update to return to normal.
Cancellation, process exit and closed host handles cannot undo work already
attempted on the pedal. Each physical phase requires a separate current review.
